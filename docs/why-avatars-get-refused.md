# Why is my Muse avatar refused?

Muse refuses an avatar request when the brief is vague, when it describes a weapon or violence, a real person, a trademarked character, or sexualised content involving a minor.

## The triggers, in plain words

- **Vague words.** "Stunning", "beautiful", "shorter" and "sexier" leave the generator to fill the gaps. It fills them wrong, or it refuses outright. Replace each vague word with a concrete detail: a defined face, a defined shape, a named garment.
- **Weapons and violence.** A brief that describes a weapon or a violent character is refused. Removing the weapon and keeping the character is the rewrite that passes.
- **Real people.** A brief that names a real person or a celebrity is refused. Describe an original character instead.
- **Trademarked characters.** A brief that names a trademarked character is refused. Describe an original character instead.
- **"Shortest possible".** A length with no floor is refused. A mini dress is the shortest length the generator allows. Move one step at a time: floor length to knee length, knee length to mini.
- **The word "style".** Writing the word "style" in a brief flips the render to a cartoon look. Name the outfit change instead.
- **Stacked escalations.** Several bolder elements in one brief get refused together, and you never learn which one caused it. Change one element at a time from the last look that worked.

## What to do when a brief is refused

Report the refusal plainly, keep the current look, and do not reword and retry the same request. Fix the brief for the next independent request: name one concrete change, and test that change on its own.
